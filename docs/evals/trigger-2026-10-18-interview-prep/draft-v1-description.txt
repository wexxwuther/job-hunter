Prepares a job seeker for an interview on a job they applied to. Reads the tracker entry, the documents they actually sent, and confirmed lessons, then builds a stage-specific prep pack (recruiter screen, hiring manager, skills test, panel, case, final round): likely questions mapped to their real STAR examples, honest gap answers that never invent experience, questions to ask, sourced company research, and a mock interview. Every claim about the user passes the family anti-fabrication gate. Use whenever the candidate has an interview, phone screen, or panel coming up for a job they applied to, even a quick ask: prep, research the company, practice answers, a mock interview, or sounding experienced in a skill they lack. Do not use for the hiring side (interview plans, questions, or scorecards for candidates), media or podcast interviews, post-interview thank-you notes or status updates (use application-tracker), salary negotiation, editing the resume (use resume-tailor), or cover letters (use cover-letter).
